# Project provenance and credits

- [Original OMEGA](https://github.com/voluptuary/dssmm/blob/dssmm/ezfomega/CREDITS.md)

Merkin Mix is maintained by Merkin / voluptuary. See those records for the contributions of FrankieT19, Sterophonick, the EZ-FLASH team, exploitz86, SkillerCMP, aidiadayo, Kekun, ChaN and other contributors. Kernel-input baselines are recorded in projects.json. Platform hardware protocols remain independent.

Development GB/GBC builds bundle [MiGoomba](https://github.com/michyprima/migoomba)
by **michyprima**, based on **Jaga / EvilJagaGenius**, **Dwedit** and **FluBBa**.
Upstream also acknowledges **Minucce**, **Nuvie**, **Radimerry**,
**Therealteamplayer** and **Endrift / mGBA**, alongside Sterophonick/EZ-FLASH.
See each project's docs/plugins/MIGOOMBA.md for pinned source and validation.

SuperFW is kept as a separate GPL-3.0-or-later project; its code and hardware protocols are not part of Merkin Mix.
